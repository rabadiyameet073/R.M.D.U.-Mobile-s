# Skill: Design System (RMDU Mobiles Platform Look & Feel)

Global authority for look, feel, typography, and visual language across the RMDU Mobiles platform.

---

## Brand Palette & Semantic Roles

- **Canvas Background**: Deep obsidian (`#08080A`) — delivers distraction-free focus on device specs.
- **Card & Surface Backgrounds**: Base slate (`#0F0F14`) with secondary hover elevation (`#16161E`).
- **Hairline Dividers & Borders**: Subtle translucent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`).
- **Category Semantic Accents**:
  - **Budget Killer**: Emerald (`#10B981`) — represents value, thrift, and efficiency.
  - **Gaming Guru**: Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4`) — represents thermal cooling, high FPS, and speed.
  - **Camera Champ**: Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`) — represents studio lighting, golden hour, and natural skin tones.
  - **Battery Boss**: Royal Purple (`#8B5CF6`) — represents power, endurance, and charging energy.
  - **G.O.A.T**: Sovereign Gold (`#EAB308`) — represents top-tier flagship luxury and peerless craftsmanship.
- **Typography Colors**:
  - Primary text: Crisp white (`#F8FAFC`).
  - Secondary text: Readable slate (`#94A3B8`).
  - Muted captions: Subtle steel (`#64748B`).

---

## Typography Hierarchy

- **Headings (Display / H1 / H2)**: Modern geometric sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Outfit`). Bold, tight tracking (`tracking-tight`), crisp contrast.
- **Body & Editorial Text**: Highly legible neutral sans (`Inter`). Clean line height (`leading-relaxed` on device reviews, `leading-normal` on cards).
- **Hardware & Metric Values**: Monospaced font (`JetBrains Mono`). Used for AnTuTu scores, mAh capacity, charging wattage (`120W`), display refresh (`144Hz`), and prices (`₹24,999` / `$299`).

---

## High-Trust Technology Demeanor

- **Tone & Demeanor**: Absolute technological objectivity, benchmark verification, and calm editorial authority (Linear / Apple / GSMArena caliber).
- **Strict Invariants**:
  - Zero scammy flash sale counters, fake "only 2 left" stock alerts, or loud neon discount badges.
  - Benchmark scores must represent real thermal performance, not short burst numbers.
  - All interactive buttons must have visible hover, active, and focus-visible keyboard outlines.

---

## Good / Bad

- ✅ Obsidian background (`#08080A`) with slate card surfaces (`#0F0F14`) and category-coded spec chips.
- ❌ Garish purple popups flashing "Claim Your Discount Now!"
- ✅ Pairing `Plus Jakarta Sans` headings with `Inter` body text and `JetBrains Mono` for hardware metrics.
- ❌ Using system default serif fonts or mixing four conflicting font families.
