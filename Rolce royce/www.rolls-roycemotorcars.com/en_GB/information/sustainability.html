SUSTAINABILITY AT
ROLLS-ROYCE MOTOR CARS

CRAFTED WITH PURPOSE

At Rolls-Royce Motor Cars, we are mindful of everything we do. From unparalleled design, craftsmanship, and attention to detail, to relationships with clients, employees, suppliers, and local communities, we strive to reduce the environmental impact of our design, engineering, and manufacturing processes.

Sustainability is an important consideration for every aspect of our operations at Rolls-Royce Motor Cars, including accreditation to international standards for environmental management. The Home of Rolls-Royce at Goodwood, West Sussex, was designed to be low-impact and entirely sympathetic to its surroundings; we are applying the same approach to our new site extension.

SUSTAINABILITY STRATEGY

The Rolls-Royce Motor Cars sustainability strategy is based on the Environmental Social Governance (ESG) model. This focuses on using resources wisely and reducing our environmental impact through management and production processes accredited to international standards. The Home of Rolls-Royce at Goodwood is environmentally compliant with ISO 14001 for Environmental Management Systems and ISO 45001 for Occupational Health and Safety management systems.

ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY

Our Environmental strategy includes how we use energy, water and materials, reduce emissions and protect and enhance biodiversity at the Home of Rolls-Royce at Goodwood. It also informs future product strategy and other long-term projects, including our site extension.

WATER

We recirculate the water used when we ‘rainwater test’ our finished motor cars and we use water-efficient technologies such as aerator taps. To reduce the use of drinking-grade water, lawns are first irrigated with extracted groundwater. Lavatories within the new site extension buildings will use harvested rainwater.

WASTE & CIRCULARITY

We’re focused on waste and circularity at the Home of Rolls-Royce. Our aim is to move items up the waste hierarchy. This involves reviewing what we reuse, refurbish, remake, and recycle, ideally keeping them in the production cycle so they never become waste at all.

BIODIVERSITY

The Home of Rolls-Royce at Goodwood supports a range of habitats, such as wildflower meadows, woodland, hedgerows, and lakes. These ecosystems are home to a wide variety of birds, animals, insects, and plants. Surveys have identified several rare and protected species, and measures are in place to ensure these are properly managed. A large sedum roof covers the current site. We also have six beehives, which are home to 250,000 English Honeybees, and an on-site Wildlife Garden, which is in the process of being rewilded. Beyond our own boundaries, a sustainability partnership with the neighbouring Goodwood Estate has been in place since 2022. Together, we’ve planted over 60,000 trees; funded a wildflower harvester and supported in the creation and restoration of ponds on site. We continue to work with the Goodwood Estate as part of our sustainability partnership. Rolls-Royce associates have been involved in setting up dormouse boxes, gathering seeds and replanting tree seedlings.

PARTS PACKAGING

We work with our suppliers to reduce the use of one-way packaging and increase the use of recycled materials. We are also increasing the amount of recycled materials used in our own logistics containers.

WASTE OPTIMISATION

A working group with members from each technology is focused on changing processes and procedures to reduce waste generated onsite, increase recycling, and cut waste going for incineration and landfill. We aim to have zero waste to landfill by the end of 2028.

WASTE COLLECTION

We work with specialist contractors to ensure the correct segregation of waste and continually review the processing of waste streams to prioritise reusing, refurbishing, and recycling materials.

PRODUCT STRATEGY

Spectre represents the first step on Rolls-Royce Motor Cars electrification journey. Each new product is subject to strict sustainability targets, including the proportion of recycled materials, such as steel and aluminium, used in our motor cars.

MATERIALS

All our wood comes from traceable, responsible sources and complies with the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (CITES) as well as the EU Deforestation Regulation. All leather at Rolls-Royce Motor Cars is sourced exclusively from European suppliers and produced to high welfare standards. We are members of the leather working group and are constantly seeking ways to reduce the environmental impact of leather production. Around 70% of the aluminium residue from our pressing plants is reused. We’re also increasing the proportion of primary materials which are created using renewable energy.

SITE EXTENSION

Rolls-Royce Motor Cars is currently investing more than £300 million to extend the manufacturing facility at Goodwood. Sustainability is a key focus, and all plans take full account of the site’s unique, beautiful and sensitive location. We’re creating habitats including hedgerows, woodland, mixed scrub and grassland, planting native trees and bushes, and seeding an earth wall which will be allowed to germinate naturally. This will help us achieve all Biodiversity Net Gain (BNG) requirements. For the first time, we will be generating our own renewable electricity onsite with the installation of a substantial photovoltaic array as part of a bio-solar roof and air-source heat pumps for low-temperature heating.

Further Information

As a wholly owned company within BMW Group, we’re fully committed to meeting its sustainability vision and targets.

FAQs

Do you offer factory tours of Goodwood?

Unfortunately, we no longer offer a public tour programme. Should this change in the future, we will update our website. 

Can I have a brochure?

All brochures can be viewed and downloaded within the model pages online or within enquiries. We no longer produce printed brochures.

I am interested in pursuing a career within Rolls-Royce Motor Cars, how can I apply?

All current opportunities are advertised on the careers page. Should you wish to register your interest or submit an application, please visit the careers page and follow the instructions. Vacancies for Apprenticeships and Internships are also advertised here. 

 

Do you offer work experience placements?

Regrettably, due to the volume of requests we receive for work experience placements from the children of our employees, we are unable to offer a placement at this time.

Looking further to the future, we offer a range of Apprenticeships and a Graduate Scheme, details of which can be found on the careers page on our website.

I would like to collaborate with Rolls-Royce Motor Cars

Unfortunately, due the volume of request we receive of this nature we cannot accept collaboration requests. 

 

I have some designs or ideas that I would like to show Rolls-Royce, who can I speak to?

We receive many requests for submission of design ideas and we are not permitted to accept ideas from any persons outside of the business. 

I need information about my classic Rolls-Royce (built prior to 2003)

All motorcars built before 2003 (both Rolls-Royce and Bentley) and their related history are dealt with by the Bentley organisation. The Bentley Heritage website could be a useful source of information for you.

If you have any questions about Rolls-Royce motor cars built after 2003 please get in touch with us on the details below and one of our client specialists will be happy to assist you.

Email: enquiries@rolls-roycemotorcars.com
Telephone: 01243 525700

How can I request sponsorship from Rolls-Royce Motor Cars?

We receive many requests for sponsorship, funding and donations, and whilst we are a keen supporter of local charities sadly we cannot support every worthy cause which is brought to our attention. Rolls-Royce Motor Cars selects a house charity each year to be our primary charitable focus, for 2019 the charity we have chosen is Home-Start Arun.

Home-Start Arun promotes the welfare of families in the Arun, Worthing and Adur District Council areas, to alleviate stress and conflict at home and prevent family crisis and breakdown. 

Can you give me the prices of your models?

The prices of our model range would depend on the specification. For further information please contact your nearest Rolls-Royce dealership who will be delighted to assist you further.
