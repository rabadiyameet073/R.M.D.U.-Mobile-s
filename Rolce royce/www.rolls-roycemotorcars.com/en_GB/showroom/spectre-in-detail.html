SPECTRE

A triumph of
design and performance

Lines of captivating clarity. A drive that goes beyond.
Spectre is a work of art — a super coupé that commands the summit of elegance, agility, and grace.

Masterful curvature

A sweeping fastback reminiscent of the world’s most evocative watercraft. This seamless surface contributes to Spectre’s drag coefficient — the lowest of any Rolls-Royce.

Following the roofline back, the vertical brightwork of the jewel-like tail lamps pay homage to the upright vanes of the Illuminated Grille. Rolls-Royce designers refer to the rear lights as an ‘island of detail’ for how their smooth panels rise gracefully out from Spectre’s rear silhouette, creating a single meeting point where all lines of the elegant fastback design converge.

Sculpted to perfection

Spectre’s enchanting presence is articulated through lines of sculptural purity. Its graceful form is exciting in every sense; the fender line evokes dynamism, while the lower waft line — inspired by yacht design — sweeps upward at the front like the gently lifting bow of an accelerating boat. This line also creates the illusion of motion even when Spectre is still, reflecting its athletic character.

An evolution of iconography

Iconic Rolls-Royce details, reinvented for this modern masterpiece. 

SPIRIT OF ECSTASY, REDESIGNED

The Spirit of Ecstasy embodies the marques audacious and intrepid sense of evolution. Her iconic form has seen many iterations since her inception in 1904,  when the sculptor Charles Robinson Sykes debuted the first of numerous interpretations. For Spectre, the iconic figurine undergoes yet another transformation. The new design depicts her riding the wind at a lower stance, her reimagined robes flow delicately in her wake. These subtle enhancements modernise our emblem; an aerodynamic muse — for who speed is an intrinsic extension of her grace.

ILLUMINATED PANTHEON GRILLE

In the full light of day, Spectre is spellbinding. Its formidable  beauty and charisma command  instant recognition and respect.  The Illuminated Pantheon Grille expands upon this notion, ensuring the motor car’s enchanting presence is equally felt after dark. The glowing grille is the widest of any Rolls-Royce. Its metal vanes are meticulously angled; the effect ‘shapes’ the air and bends the light, creating a wondrous radiance that draws observers in.

iconic headlights

Rolls-Royce’s iconic split headlights have not been seen since Spectre’s spiritual predecessor, the Phantom Coupé. Spectre builds upon the original, visually arresting design with two components — upper daytime running lights and a lower, four-lamp cluster that brightly illuminates the road ahead.

The apex of athleticism
and elegance

Spectre is an emblem of agility and grace, engineered with precise handling and a fast-accelerating drive. This dynamism is matched with effortless performance and remarkable control.

A canvas for individual expression

Owners are invited to transform Spectre’s flawless surfaces into Bespoke masterpieces.

At the Home of Rolls-Royce in Goodwood, master artisans bring each vision to life, turning dreams into exquisite reality. Explore an endless palette of paint possibilities, accentuate Spectre’s form with a vivid Coachline, or infuse its woodwork with intricate marquetry. 

The wonder within

Immerse yourself in Spectre’s modern cabin, where flawless design meets superior comfort. 

A CANOPY OF STARS

Rolls-Royce’s Starlight Headliner has been a statement feature since its 2007 debut, transfixing passengers with the atmospheric glow and hues of delicately placed fibre-optic lights. Ablaze with an intricate show of shooting stars, this stellar sky shines down on Spectre’s contemporary cabin.

A CASCADE OF LIGHT

Upon starting the motor car, the Illuminated Fascia awakens with 5,500 stars. The star-field swirls around the Spectre nameplate, delicately encasing it in vibrant light.

TEXTURED MATERIALS

Spectre’s doors serve as the ultimate canvas for canadel panelling. The veneer is slip-matched at a precise 55° angle, creating a seamless grain-pattern that retains the wood’s natural texture.

SUPERIOR SEATING

Spectre’s rear seat is a masterclass in what occurs when a standard concept is pushed by the world-class designers, engineers, and artisans at the Home of Rolls-Royce in Goodwood. The continuous seat design wraps itself around the passenger, cocooning them in supple leather.

A CELESTIAL ENTRANCE

A spellbinding option for the interior, Starlight Doors radiate with 4,796 backlit perforations. Each ‘star’ is positioned by hand, creating a subtle sense of motion within the cabin. Spectre’s doors are the largest of any Rolls-Royce, measuring 1.5 metres in length. Crafted with effortless technology, they open and close at the touch of a button, creating the accommodating experience of a built-in chauffeur.

DIGITAL HARMONY

SPIRIT is the digital extension of the Rolls-Royce Architecture of Luxury — setting a new precedent for how technology and luxury are married with automotive excellence.

SPIRIT manages Spectre’s functions and seamlessly integrates into Whispers, Rolls-Royce’s exclusive owners app, allowing remote interactions with your car and the delivery of live information curated by luxury intelligence specialists.

BESPOKE INSTRUMENT DIALS

Seen for the first time on any Rolls-Royce, fully digital Bespoke Instrument Dials are a stunning display of technological craftsmanship, indicative of Spectre’s digital identity and the marque’s unparalleled design proficiency.

Spectre owners may select a personalised and complementary dial colour upon commission. Modernising the view from the driver’s seat, the dials’ hands are decorated with a trail of brilliant stars — an enchanting celestial connection to the Illuminated Doors, Starlight Headliner, and Illuminated Fascia.

CONNECTED WITH WHISPERS

The Rolls-Royce experience extends beyond the physical drive. With Whispers, the owners-only app, drivers are always in tune with Spectre — the most connected Rolls-Royce yet. Once connected, drivers can send curated Whispers locations, such as restaurant recommendations, directly to the motor car for effortless navigation. Drivers can also discover their nearest charging station,  precondition the interior and securely lock and unlock Spectre from their mobile device.

YOUR INTELLIGENT PERSONAL ASSISTANT

A range of needs may arise when driving Spectre, from knowing  the whereabouts of your nearest charging station to activating  the massage seat. Rather than acting upon these yourself,  Eleanor is able to grant your requests.

SEAMLESS OWNERSHIP SERVICES EXPERIENCE

Experience the pinnacle of connectivity with Spectre. Next generation technology seamlessly integrates service partner allocation, and roadside assistant support — all from the  comfort of your vehicle or through the Whispers app.

FAQs

Do you offer factory tours of Goodwood?

Unfortunately, we no longer offer a public tour programme. Should this change in the future, we will update our website. 

Can I have a brochure?

All brochures can be viewed and downloaded within the model pages online or within enquiries. We no longer produce printed brochures.

I am interested in pursuing a career within Rolls-Royce Motor Cars, how can I apply?

All current opportunities are advertised on the careers page. Should you wish to register your interest or submit an application, please visit the careers page and follow the instructions. Vacancies for Apprenticeships and Internships are also advertised here. 

 

Do you offer work experience placements?

Regrettably, due to the volume of requests we receive for work experience placements from the children of our employees, we are unable to offer a placement at this time.

Looking further to the future, we offer a range of Apprenticeships and a Graduate Scheme, details of which can be found on the careers page on our website.

I would like to collaborate with Rolls-Royce Motor Cars

Unfortunately, due the volume of request we receive of this nature we cannot accept collaboration requests. 

 

I have some designs or ideas that I would like to show Rolls-Royce, who can I speak to?

We receive many requests for submission of design ideas and we are not permitted to accept ideas from any persons outside of the business. 

I need information about my classic Rolls-Royce (built prior to 2003)

All motorcars built before 2003 (both Rolls-Royce and Bentley) and their related history are dealt with by the Bentley organisation. The Bentley Heritage website could be a useful source of information for you.

If you have any questions about Rolls-Royce motor cars built after 2003 please get in touch with us on the details below and one of our client specialists will be happy to assist you.

Email: enquiries@rolls-roycemotorcars.com
Telephone: 01243 525700

How can I request sponsorship from Rolls-Royce Motor Cars?

We receive many requests for sponsorship, funding and donations, and whilst we are a keen supporter of local charities sadly we cannot support every worthy cause which is brought to our attention. Rolls-Royce Motor Cars selects a house charity each year to be our primary charitable focus, for 2019 the charity we have chosen is Home-Start Arun.

Home-Start Arun promotes the welfare of families in the Arun, Worthing and Adur District Council areas, to alleviate stress and conflict at home and prevent family crisis and breakdown.

The prices of our model range would depend on the specification. For further information please contact your nearest Rolls-Royce dealership who will be delighted to assist you further.

General Enquiry SPECTRE - IN DETAIL

ARCHITECTURE OF LUXURY

Built upon the Architecture of Luxury — a custom spaceframe that enables adaptive engineering and peerless  ride quality — Spectre combines agile performance with the marque’s signature Magic Carpet Ride.

UNPRECEDENTED INTELLIGENCE

Intuitive, perceptive, and sharp, Spectre is the most connected Rolls-Royce the marque has ever envisioned. With 141,200 sender-receiver functions, seven kilometres of cabling, and over 25,000 sub-functions, it is able to process complex data and rapidly respond to driver inputs and changing road conditions.

PLANAR SUSPENSION SYSTEM

Engineers describe Spectre’s suspension as ‘an orchestra of systems’ that combines high-speed processing with specifically developed hardware. It independently maintains perfect tracking, stability and cornering, contributing to the effortless Magic Carpet Ride sensation.

DOMINATE THE ROAD

Spectre’s Architecture of Luxury fits formidable 23-inch wheels, which are impressive from both an aesthetic and performance perspective. For drives who find themselves on icy, snow-capped surfaces, coupling the wheels with winter tyres provides perfect control and stability.
